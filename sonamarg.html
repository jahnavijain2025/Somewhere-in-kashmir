04 / THE MEADOW OF GOLD

Sonamarg

Glaciers, alpine rivers & wild mountain landscapes.

Explore Sonamarg ↓

WHERE THE ROAD MEETS THE HIGH HIMALAYAS

Follow the mountains into the wild.

Sonamarg is known for its broad alpine valley, glacier excursions and access to high-altitude trekking country. Snow, road access and transport arrangements vary throughout the year, so check current local advice before planning excursions to remote areas.

PLACES TO DISCOVER

Make time for these.

01 / THE GLACIER TRAIL

Thajiwas Glacier

A popular excursion from Sonamarg, Thajiwas is known for its snowfields and dramatic mountain scenery. The route and available activities depend on season and conditions; check local access and use authorised guides or transport where required.

Explore Sonamarg photos on Wikimedia Commons
02 / THE HIGH MOUNTAIN PASS

Zoji La Pass & Zero Point

Zoji La is a dramatic high-altitude pass on the route between Kashmir and Ladakh. Snow, weather and road conditions can change rapidly, and travel may be restricted. Confirm current road status and authorised transport before planning this excursion.

Explore Baltal photos on Wikimedia Commons
03 / THE ALPINE VALLEY

Baltal Valley

Baltal lies beyond Sonamarg on the route towards Zoji La and is known as a base area for the Amarnath pilgrimage. Its high mountain setting is striking, but access can be affected by weather, security and pilgrimage arrangements.

Explore river photos on Wikimedia Commons
04 / THE RIVER VALLEY

Sindh River

The Sindh River runs through the Sonamarg valley, adding movement and sound to the landscape. Enjoy the river from safe viewing spots and follow local guidance around fast currents and slippery banks.

Explore Nilagrad photos on Wikimedia Commons
05 / COLOURED WATERS

Nilagrad River

Nilagrad is known for its distinctive reddish appearance in some stretches and its location in the wider Sonamarg region. Verify the route, local access and conditions before travelling.

Photo source: Wikimedia Commons
06 / THE HIGH-ALTITUDE LAKES

Gangabal Lake

Gangabal is a celebrated alpine lake beneath Mount Harmukh and is reached by trekking rather than a simple roadside visit. This demanding mountain journey requires preparation, weather checks and route planning.

Photo source: Wikimedia Commons
07 / THE TREKKING ROUTE

Kashmir Great Lakes Trek

A multi-day trek through alpine meadows, high passes and glacial lakes, this route is best suited to trekkers with appropriate fitness and preparation. The trail is seasonal and should be undertaken with a reliable operator and current route guidance.
