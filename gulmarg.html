02 / THE ALPINE ESCAPE

Gulmarg

Meadows, snow peaks & mountain adventures.

Explore Gulmarg ↓

WHERE THE MOUNTAINS OPEN UP

A landscape made for adventure.

From the famous Gondola ride to high-altitude lakes and quiet heritage landmarks, Gulmarg changes with the seasons. Visit for snow-covered slopes in winter or green meadows and mountain walks in warmer months. Check weather, transport and activity availability before setting out.

PLACES TO DISCOVER

Make time for these.

01 / ABOVE THE MEADOWS

Gulmarg Gondola

Ride one of Kashmir's best-known cable cars for sweeping views of the surrounding mountains. The ride operates in stages and may be affected by weather, so check ticket availability and operating conditions before visiting.

View photo source
02 / HIGH-ALTITUDE VIEWS

Apharwat Peak

A high mountain destination reached via the upper Gondola stage when services are operating. The landscape can be snowy and dramatic, but conditions change quickly. Follow local guidance and do not venture beyond permitted areas.

View photo source
03 / THE ALPINE LAKE

Alpather Lake

A high-altitude lake near the Apharwat range, known for its striking mountain setting. The route is seasonal and can be demanding, so check trail conditions and use a local guide when appropriate.

View Gulmarg photo sources
04 / OPEN GREEN SPACES

Gulmarg Golf Course

Set among broad meadows and tall trees, Gulmarg's golf course is a beautiful place to appreciate the open landscape. Access and playing arrangements depend on the season and local operations.

View photo source
05 / WATER & ROCK

Drung Waterfall

A popular waterfall excursion near the Gulmarg area, especially atmospheric when the surrounding landscape is covered in snow. Water flow and winter access vary by season; confirm road and local transport conditions before going.

View photo source
06 / COLONIAL HERITAGE

St. Mary's Church

A historic church dating to the early twentieth century, surrounded by Gulmarg's alpine scenery. Its stone exterior and peaceful setting offer a different side of the destination beyond outdoor adventures.

View photo source
07 / SACRED LANDMARK

Maharani Temple

Also known as the Shiva Temple, this distinctive shrine stands on a small rise in Gulmarg and is visible from many parts of the meadow. It is a memorable stop for its architecture and setting.

View photo source
08 / SPIRITUAL HERITAGE

Baba Reshi Shrine

A revered shrine known for its traditional architecture and peaceful surroundings. Located below the Gulmarg area, it can be included as a heritage stop when road and visitor access are suitable.
