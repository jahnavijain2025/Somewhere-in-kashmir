A DIFFERENT SIDE OF KASHMIR

Some places ask you to slow down.

These escapes are about more than ticking off attractions. They are about quiet roads, mountain air, riverside pauses and landscapes that feel wonderfully removed from the rush. Some locations require longer journeys, permits or seasonal access, so plan ahead and respect local guidance.

THE QUIETER SIDE OF THE VALLEY

Discover somewhere different.

Photo: Wikimedia Commons
01 / MEADOWS & STREAMS

Doodhpathri

Known as the Valley of Milk, Doodhpathri is a green meadow destination surrounded by rolling hills, forests and flowing streams. It is a lovely place for a scenic walk, a picnic and a slower day in nature.

Travel note: Check road conditions and local access, particularly during winter or heavy rainfall.
Representative Kashmir landscape: Wikimedia Commons
02 / THE QUIET ESCAPE

Chatpal

Chatpal is a peaceful destination in south Kashmir known for its forested surroundings, mountain scenery and rural atmosphere. It suits travellers who prefer quiet landscapes and unhurried time away from busy tourist centres.

Photo note: This is a general Kashmir valley image, not a verified photograph of Chatpal itself. Arrange transport in advance and confirm local access.
Photo: Wikimedia Commons
03 / COUNTRYSIDE & ORCHARDS

Lolab Valley

Lolab Valley offers a gentler side of Kashmir, with open countryside, orchards, villages and wooded mountain slopes. Take time to appreciate the rural scenery and learn about the region through respectful interactions with local communities.

Travel note: Confirm local travel advice and accommodation before setting out.
Photo: Wikimedia Commons
04 / REMOTE MOUNTAIN COUNTRY

Gurez Valley

Gurez is a remote mountain valley associated with traditional villages, rugged peaks and the Kishanganga River. Its distinctive landscape and quieter pace make it a memorable longer journey for travellers seeking a different side of Kashmir.

Travel note: Check current security and road guidance. Permit or registration requirements may apply; verify the latest rules before travelling.
Photo: Wikimedia Commons
05 / FORESTS & MOUNTAIN ROADS

Daksum & Breng Valley

The Daksum area and the wider Breng Valley are known for forested hills, streams and scenic drives through south Kashmir. This is a good choice for travellers who enjoy the journey itself as much as the destination.

Travel note: Weather can affect mountain roads. Check conditions before driving and avoid unfamiliar routes after dark.
Photo: Wikimedia Commons
06 / A QUIETER LAKESIDE

Nigeen Lake

Just beyond Srinagar's better-known lake experiences, Nigeen offers a quieter atmosphere with houseboats, shikaras and beautiful water reflections. Spend a morning by the shore or enjoy a peaceful boat ride.

Travel note: This lakeside escape is near Srinagar, so it can fit easily into a city itinerary.
