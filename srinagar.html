01 / THE LAKESIDE ESCAPE

Srinagar

Lakes, gardens & timeless charm.

Explore the city ↓

THE HEART OF KASHMIR

A city best experienced slowly.

From the gentle movement of shikaras on Dal Lake to the terraced Mughal gardens overlooking the water, Srinagar brings together nature, history and everyday Kashmiri life. Leave time to wander, enjoy the views and experience the city beyond a checklist of attractions.

PLACES TO DISCOVER

Make time for these.

Photo: Wikimedia Commons
01 / LAKE EXPERIENCE

Dal Lake

Take a shikara ride across Srinagar's iconic lake, admire the traditional houseboats and watch the mountains reflect on the water. The Floating Vegetable Market is an early-morning experience, often visited around 5 AM; confirm local arrangements before going.

Photo: Wikimedia Commons — Nigeen Lake
02 / QUIETER WATERS

Nigeen Lake

A quieter alternative to the busier parts of Dal Lake, Nigeen is known for its houseboats, shikaras and relaxed lakeside atmosphere. Spend time here if you prefer a slower pace and a more peaceful view of Srinagar.

Photo: Wikimedia Commons
03 / MUGHAL HERITAGE

Nishat Bagh

Known as the Garden of Joy, Nishat Bagh is a historic terraced garden on the eastern side of Dal Lake. Walk past its landscaped terraces, water channels and flower beds, with views towards the lake and the surrounding Zabarwan hills.

Photo: Wikimedia Commons
04 / ROYAL GARDENS

Shalimar Bagh

Built by Mughal emperor Jahangir, Shalimar Bagh is celebrated for its formal garden layout, tree-lined paths, water channels and historic pavilions. It offers a glimpse into Kashmir's Mughal-era garden design.

Photo: Wikimedia Commons
05 / THE ROYAL SPRING

Chashme Shahi

This compact Mughal garden is associated with a natural spring and sits in the Zabarwan range above Dal Lake. Its terraces, greenery and mountain setting make it a lovely stop on a garden-focused day in Srinagar.

Photo: Wikimedia Commons
06 / PANORAMIC VIEWS

Pari Mahal

Pari Mahal is a seven-terraced historic monument perched on the Zabarwan hills above Dal Lake. Explore its arched architecture and garden terraces, then enjoy sweeping views across Srinagar.

Photo: Wikimedia Commons
07 / HISTORY & SPIRITUALITY

Shankaracharya Temple

Dedicated to Lord Shiva, this historic hilltop temple stands above Srinagar and offers expansive views of the city and Dal Lake. Check current access, opening arrangements and visitor rules before your visit.

Photo: Wikimedia Commons
08 / HISTORY & SPIRITUALITY

Hazratbal Shrine

Located on the northern bank of Dal Lake, Hazratbal is an important Muslim shrine known for its white marble architecture and spiritual significance. Dress respectfully and follow local visitor guidance.

Photo: Wikimedia Commons
09 / NATURE & WILDLIFE

Dachigam National Park

A protected Himalayan landscape near Srinagar, Dachigam is known for its forests, mountain scenery and the endangered hangul, also called the Kashmir stag. Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed; check access and guided-visit options before planning.

Photo: Wikimedia Commons
10 / SEASONAL BEAUTY

Indira Gandhi Memorial Tulip Garden

Set against the Zabarwan hills, this garden is famous for its colourful spring tulip displays. It is a seasonal attraction, so check the current bloom and opening dates before planning your visit.
