03 / THE VALLEY OF SHEPHERDS

Pahalgam

Rivers, pine forests & endless green valleys.

Explore Pahalgam ↓

WHERE THE RIVER MEETS THE FOREST

Let the valley set the pace.

Pahalgam is a landscape of pine-covered slopes, open meadows and the clear waters of the Lidder River. Spend time exploring its valleys, take in the mountain air and plan excursions according to weather, road access and local guidance.

PLACES TO DISCOVER

Make time for these.

Photo source: Wikimedia Commons
01 / THE FAMOUS VALLEY

Betaab Valley

Known for its broad meadows, surrounding mountains and the Lidder River, Betaab Valley is a classic Pahalgam landscape. Walk through the scenic area, enjoy the views and check local entry arrangements before visiting.

Photo source: Wikimedia Commons
02 / ALPINE MEADOWS

Aru Valley

Aru is a peaceful mountain village and a starting point for several trekking routes. Its open meadows, pine forests and surrounding peaks make it ideal for nature walks and a slower day away from busy tourist areas.

Photo source: Wikimedia Commons
03 / THE MEADOWS

Baisaran Hills

Often nicknamed Mini Switzerland, Baisaran is known for its open meadow and forest-framed mountain views. **Important:** access can change due to security conditions. Confirm current official guidance and whether the area is open before planning a visit.

Photo source: Wikimedia Commons
04 / THE MOUNTAIN ROAD

Chandanwari

Chandanwari is a mountain destination on the route associated with the Amarnath Yatra. The drive offers dramatic scenery, and the surrounding landscape changes significantly with the season. Check road access and weather before travelling.

Photo source: Wikimedia Commons
05 / HERITAGE & SPIRITUALITY

Mamaleshwar Temple

A historic Shiva temple in Pahalgam, Mamaleshwar offers a quieter cultural stop amid the valley's natural scenery. Visitors should respect the religious setting and follow local customs.

Photo source: Wikimedia Commons
06 / THE RIVER LIFE

Lidder River

The fast-flowing Lidder River runs through the Pahalgam landscape, bringing sound and movement to the valley. Enjoy the riverside scenery from safe designated areas and keep away from slippery banks or strong currents.
